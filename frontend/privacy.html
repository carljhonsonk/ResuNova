Privacy

ResuNova is a student project (Version 1). This page says plainly what happens to your resume.

What happens to your resume

  • Your file is read in the server’s memory to produce the analysis.
  • The file and its text are never written to disk. An uploaded file is deleted after the analysis, or after ten minutes at the latest.
  • There is no account, no login, and no advertising or analytics on this site.

What is saved

  • Only the derived result: the role you chose, the score, the list of skills found, the problems found and the roadmap.
  • Sentences copied from your resume (for the rewrite suggestions) are not saved.
  • On free hosting, saved results may disappear whenever the site restarts.

What the server notes

  • To stop abuse, the server counts requests per IP address, in memory only, for ten minutes.
  • The hosting provider may keep its own technical logs (for example, request addresses). ResuNova does not log resume contents.

Please note

  • ResuNova gives guidance, not a guarantee. It cannot predict whether any employer will select you.
  • Do not upload a resume that contains information you are not comfortable sharing with a student project. A sample resume is fine.
